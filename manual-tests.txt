# Module 6: Ticket 602 - Use neutral colors

## Test steps
1. Build and run CiCo.
2. Enter 12345678 into the field and select the whole number.
3. Replace the number with 00000000 and click Update.
4. Wait 11 seconds.
5. Enter 00000000 again and click Update.
6. Wait 11 seconds.
7. Enter 99999999 and click Update.
8. Click OK.
9. Enter 99999999 again, click Update, and wait 11 seconds.
10. Click the X to close the window.

## Expected results
* The main, status, and error panels should use a light gray background with dark text.
* The input field should be white. The buttons and selected text background should be gray.
* The text, selected number, and blinking cursor should be easy to see.
* The status panel should display "Developer" and either "Checked IN" or "Checked OUT" after step 3.
* The status should switch to the opposite value after step 5.
* The main panel should return with an empty field after steps 4, 6, 8, and 9.
* The error panel should display "Please show your card to staff to validate." after step 7.
* The version number should stay visible in white on a dark gray footer.
* The application should stop and display "Clean shutdown" in the console after step 10.
